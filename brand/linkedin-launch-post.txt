EPANetLab is LIVE — water modeling, right in your browser. Free, fast, local-first.

Working as a water/wastewater/drainage design engineer, I've always felt the gap between powerful EPANET calculations and software that's actually pleasant to use. So I built my own answer:

• Draw junctions, pipes, tanks, pumps and valves — visually, with snap-to-pipe
• Import .inp, shapefiles and DXF plans (fillets, arcs, splines included)
• Run steady-state & extended-period simulations instantly — EPANET engine in WebAssembly, no installs
• See pressure, flow, velocity, head color-coded straight on the map
• Export back to .inp — fully interoperable with the EPANET ecosystem

No logins. No cloud required. Your models stay on your machine.

👉 Open it today: https://app.epanetlab.com

Built for engineers, utilities, educators and students. I'd love your feedback — try it, break it, tell me what to fix next.
